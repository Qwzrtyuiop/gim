# 6. Overall Narrative Structure

The planned story of Gulong Ng Buhay explores the life of a man who tried to escape poverty, briefly achieved financial success, and lost everything through a combination of corporate wrongdoing, financial hardship, and destructive personal decisions.

Returning to his hometown forces John to confront both his present circumstances and the unresolved relationships he left behind.

Through his work as a tricycle driver, John encounters people from different professions, social backgrounds, and circumstances. Their stories gradually reveal different aspects of the community, the city's institutions, and John's own history.

The three routes examine these experiences through different perspectives:

| Aspect | Route 1 | Route 2 | Route 3 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Primary focus | John's new life | City corruption | Society and John's past |
| Narrative direction | Rebuilding | Investigating | Discovering and confronting |
| Accessibility | Default | Relationship threshold | Hidden requirements |
| Isidro's role | Disappearance unexplained | Death is a major plot point | Death is preventable |
| Father's mystery | Not established as central | Not established as central | Major revelations |

Although the routes share the same world, characters, and fixed historical events, the player's decisions determine which stories continue, what information becomes available, and which outcomes can change.

The central idea is that John's life is shaped not only by what has happened to him, but also by the people he chooses to become involved with after returning home.

The story's ultimate endings, the complete route requirements, and the precise resolution of the father's disappearance remain to be detailed.
